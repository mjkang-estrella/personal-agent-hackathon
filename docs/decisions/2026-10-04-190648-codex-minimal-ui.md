# Warm minimal transition interface

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status: accepted
- Owner: Codex, implementing Nolan's requested 简洁风 redesign

## Context

The dashboard's introductory copy, employer journey, metric cards, decorative panels, and colorful task cards compete with the transition plan. Nolan approved a quieter visual direction and requested a new branch and PR.

## Decision

Keep the three-stage board and existing evidence/approval drawers. Replace the journey and metric cards with compact dates and task counts. Remove decorative panels and duplicate assistant entry points. Use warm neutral surfaces, one font family, restrained green, readable labels, and subtle borders. Keep task descriptions in the detail drawer and source counts on cards.

Remove the aggregate benefits figure: it adds policy allowances and potential claims, which are not interchangeable or confirmed receipts. Preserve individual amounts and their qualifying labels. Preserve submitted, approved/unpaid, and completed states, source evidence, and explicit submission controls.

## Rationale

A quieter hierarchy makes deadlines and required reviews easier to find. This is a presentation change within the user-approved direction, not a change to benefit eligibility or workflow authorization.

## Consequences

No new dependencies, API contracts, schema changes, or provider behavior. Existing responsive board stacking remains; compact navigation retains accessible labels and settings access. Detailed descriptions require opening a task.
